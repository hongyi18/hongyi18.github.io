## Selected musical moments

### From Naples to China

*Notte d'estate: Canti da Napoli alla Cina* brought Italian and Chinese music together in Contursi Terme. I performed with soprano Dr. Xia Liu, soprano Katerina Furstova, and physicist and pianist Massimo Blasone, a professor at the University of Salerno. The program spanned Italian and French opera arias, classical and Neapolitan songs, and Chinese folk songs and art songs.

[Concert poster ↗](/personal/canti_da_napoli_alla_cina.jpg) · [View in archive →](/activities/#music-2026-07-naples-china)

### Fairy tales and wisdom

At this German and Austrian art-song salon in Shanghai, hosted by soprano Dr. Xia Liu, I performed with pianist and tenor Yinhe Fang. My performances included Schubert's *Die Forelle*, D. 550, *Erlkönig*, D. 328, and *Der Zwerg*, D. 771; three arias from Mozart's *Die Zauberflöte*; and Beethoven's *An die Freude*.
