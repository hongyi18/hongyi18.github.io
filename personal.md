### Concerts and music salons

1. 2026.07, [Notte d'estate: Canti da Napoli alla Cina](/personal/poster_260718.jpg), @ Contursi Terme. The concert is hosted with Dr. Xia Liu (soprano), Katerina Furstova (soprano), and Dr. Massimo Blasone (physicist and pianist) and the program includes Italian opera arias, classic songs, Neapolitan songs, French opera arias, and Chinese folk songs and art songs.
1. 2025.04, [Enchanted by Shanghai Nights — A Concert of Pop, Jazz, and Tango Classics](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/I0zXM4hB3aksxNIO-8P5Sw), @ Shanghai. My performances include *Wien, du Stadt meiner Träume* and *Strahlender Mond*.
2. 2025.01, [Fairy Tales and Wisdom — A Salon of German and Austrian Art Songs](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/hqGqkhUbOGgd1VF-oS-Nnw), @ Shanghai. The music salon is hosted by Dr. Xia Liu (soprano) and performed with Yinhe Fang (pianist and tenor). My performances include *Die Forelle D.550*, *Erlkoenig D.328*, *Der Zwerg D.771*, three arias in *Die Zauberfloete*, and *An die Freude*.
3. 2023.01, [The Plum Blossom on Snow]({% post_url 2023-01-08-the-plum-blossom-on-snow %}), @ Cologne. The concert is hosted with Xia Liu (soprano) and the program includes Chinese art songs, folk songs, and piano music.
